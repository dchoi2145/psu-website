The Override season so far

Since August our teams have competed at eight events, six of them Signature Events, from Minnesota to Maine.

2145X and 2145V won Bruin in the Den together, 2145Y took the Create Award at Smoky Mountain Forge, and 2145X is the Robot Skills Champion from the OWL Firehouse Fall Classic.
