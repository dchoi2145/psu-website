Our best season yet

The 2025–26 Push Back season brought championships at the local, state and world level.

We sent all five PSU teams to the VEX World Championship in St. Louis, Missouri, in April 2026. 2145Z won the Design division and made it to the Dome semifinals. A month earlier, 2145Z and 2145Y won the Virginia high school and middle school state championships.
